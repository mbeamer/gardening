# Hearty Vegetable Lasagna

**Makes:** 12 servings  
**Source:** [Allrecipes - Hearty Vegetable Lasagna](https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/11786/hearty-vegetable-lasagna/)

## Produce

- [ ] 1 lb fresh mushrooms
- [ ] 3/4 cup green bell pepper
- [ ] 3/4 cup onion
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic

## Pantry

- [ ] 1 (16 oz) package lasagna noodles
- [ ] 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 2 (26 oz) jars pasta sauce
- [ ] 1 tsp dried basil

## Dairy

- [ ] 1 (15 oz) container part-skim ricotta cheese
- [ ] 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
- [ ] 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Eggs

- [ ] 2 eggs

## Check your pantry

The recipe does not specify optional items. Check that you have vegetable oil and dried basil before shopping.
